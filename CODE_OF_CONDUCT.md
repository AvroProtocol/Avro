# Community Code of Conduct

AVRO welcomes respectful technical discussion and contributions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss code and ideas rather than attacking people.
- Assume good faith while still asking for evidence and clear reasoning.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, personal data, or private security reports.
- Keep technical discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, repeated personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

For community conduct concerns, contact the project maintainers privately. Security vulnerabilities should be reported separately through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and the project.
